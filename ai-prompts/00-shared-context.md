# Shared Context (applies to ALL agents)

Audience:
- Chinese university students in Australia
- Little/no Australian work experience
- Want internships/jobs but the market feels difficult and the path is unclear
- English level varies → structure must be easy to scan

Website goal:
- Personal service website: introduce me first → explain services → prove credibility with student reviews

Core site structure (must follow):
1) Home (Intro Me First)
2) Services (What I Provide + How It Helps)
3) Reviews (Student Testimonials)
Optional: Contact section + CTA blocks across pages

Language rule (IMPORTANT):
- Agent outputs (plans/specs/tasks) may be in English.
- ALL website-facing content MUST be Simplified Chinese (简体中文).
  This includes: headings, paragraphs, buttons/CTAs, form labels, testimonials, microcopy, navigation labels.
- Do NOT translate Chinese website copy into English in the deliverables.

Tone for website copy:
- Supportive, realistic, actionable, not salesy
- Short paragraphs + clear headings + bullet points
- Avoid overpromising outcomes; focus on process/support
- Testimonials must be specific: student background + situation + what we did + concrete benefit
