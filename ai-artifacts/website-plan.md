# A) Site Plan (English allowed)
## 1) Navigation
- Final nav labels (Chinese): 首页｜服务｜学员评价｜联系
- Rationale (English): Keep the primary flow (Intro → Services → Proof) in the main nav for quick scanning. Add “Contact” as an optional but persistent endpoint for CTAs across pages.

## 2) Page-by-page structure
### Home (Intro Me First)
- Purpose (English allowed): Establish who I am, who I help, and what the first step looks like—fast, clear, low-pressure.
- Key sections (H2 list, English allowed):
  - Hero
  - Who I am
  - Who I help
  - How I help (3-card overview)
  - Proof (testimonial preview)
  - CTA block
- CTA strategy (English allowed): Primary CTA = book a short intro call; secondary CTA = view services. Repeat CTA after “How I help” and after testimonials.
- Trust signals to include (English allowed): Short, specific student testimonials; clear process; realistic wording (“increase clarity”, “improve response rate”, “practice interviews”) and no guarantees.

### Services
- Purpose (English allowed): Turn interest into intent by describing concrete deliverables, a simple process, and who each service is for.
- Key sections (H2 list, English allowed):
  - Overview
  - Service cards (3–5)
  - What working together looks like (simple steps)
  - FAQ (time, language, format, what to prepare)
  - CTA block
- CTA strategy (English allowed): Each service ends with a small CTA; page ends with a stronger CTA to book an intro call.
- Trust signals to include (English allowed): Deliverables lists; example artifacts (sanitized screenshots later); “what you’ll prepare” checklist; scope boundaries.

### Reviews
- Purpose (English allowed): Provide credibility with relatable student stories (background + obstacle + what we did + benefit).
- Key sections (H2 list, English allowed):
  - Intro guidance paragraph
  - Review card grid
  - CTA block
- CTA strategy (English allowed): After ~3 cards, show a mid-page CTA; end-page CTA to book intro call.
- Trust signals to include (English allowed): Specific contexts (degree, no local exp, target roles); specific improvements (structure, clarity, interview answers) instead of “guaranteed offers”.

### Contact (optional)
- Purpose (English allowed): Provide a simple, low-friction way to reach out.
- Key sections (H2 list, English allowed):
  - Contact options (form + preferred channel)
  - What to include in your message
  - Response time expectation
- CTA strategy (English allowed): “Book intro call” + “Send a message”.
- Trust signals to include (English allowed): Clear response window; privacy note; what happens after contacting.

## 3) First-time visitor flow
- What they see first → why they trust → why they act (English allowed):
  - See: A supportive, realistic headline + “who this is for” bullets + simple 3-step help overview.
  - Trust: Specific student review snippets + clear deliverables + no overpromising.
  - Act: One low-commitment next step (15-min intro call) and a secondary option (browse services) for hesitant visitors.
