# CLAUDE.md

## Main Goal

This website exists to proudly promote a genuine passion for teaching and preparing students to find jobs in Australia.

Everything on this site should reflect that heart — honest, encouraging, and focused on student success.

## Most Important Rule

**Always ask before making any assumption. Never assume. If something is unclear, ask first.**


**Always research online on how to make better UI and the rules and what so to make the UI looks better.**

## Content & Tone

**All generated text must sound human, not AI.** Avoid generic marketing fluff, overly polished phrasing, or robotic patterns. Write like a real person would—natural, varied, sometimes imperfect. Steer clear of phrases that scream "AI-generated" (e.g., "dive into," "unlock your potential," "seamlessly," "leverage," "robust," "comprehensive solution").
